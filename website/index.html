The agent orchestrator

A calm control surface for complex work.

Allegretto keeps the plan visible, the model choice explicit, and every side effect behind a tool boundary you can inspect. It is not a chat box with hidden automation bolted on.

Orchestrate locally

The app owns the conversation, context, approvals, and run state on your machine. Stop, edit, or redirect work without waiting for a remote control plane.

Choose the boundary

Use an on-device model for private work, or connect a provider with your own key when the task needs a larger model. The active model is always named.

Make action legible

Tools have names, inputs, and approvals. Workflows show their steps and dependencies before they run, so agency remains something you can reason about.

Models, not mystery

The model is a component. The agency is yours.

Allegretto separates the model/provider boundary from the work it coordinates. Swap models without losing your tools, workflows, or local context.

  • 01

    Local models stay local

    When a model runs on your device, its prompt and response remain there. No Allegretto account or hosted inference service is required.

  • 02

    Provider calls are direct

    For a hosted model, your request goes to the provider you selected using your own credentials. Virtually(Creative) does not proxy or read it.

  • 03

    Context follows the task

    Models can propose and explain; the orchestrator decides which context, tools, and approvals belong to the next step.

Tools and workflows

From a sentence to a sequence you can see.

Ask for an outcome. Allegretto turns it into a proposed sequence, surfaces dependencies, and lets your permission setting decide what happens next.

STEP 01

Describe the outcome

Start with intent instead of a maze of menus. Keep the conversation attached to the work as the plan evolves.

STEP 02

Review the plan

See what can happen in parallel, what has to wait, and which tools each step would use. Change the plan before running it.

STEP 03

Run with agency

Choose the side effects you want, watch progress in context, and interrupt when the situation changes.

The browser bridge

Useful where the work already lives.

The browser bridge lets an agent work with an approved browser context instead of pretending the web is a text-only API. You choose when it can act.

  • ↗

    Connect an existing browser

    Keep your normal browser, profiles, and sessions. Allegretto does not need to move your work into a hosted browser.

  • ✓

    Review meaningful actions

    Reading, clicking, typing, and navigation are tool actions with visible state. Your permission setting controls when they pause for you.

  • ×

    Interrupt at any time

    When a page changes or the plan is wrong, pause the run and take control back immediately.

A deliberate data boundary

Your work should have a clear home.

On your device

Conversations, local model files, workflow state, and app preferences live on the device running Allegretto.

At your provider

Hosted-model prompts and responses travel directly to the provider you select, under its terms and privacy policy.

With Virtually(Creative)

We run the product site, not a hidden inference relay. We do not receive your model traffic or sell your work.

Your call

Choose local inference when data must stay local; choose a hosted provider when its capability is worth the explicit boundary.
